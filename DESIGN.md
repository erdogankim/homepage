## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intersection of ancient esoteric contemplative philosophy (Sufism/Tasavvuf) and hyper-modern precision sciences (quantum physics, software architecture, and computational cosmology). 

The target audience spans intellectuals, researchers, engineers, patrons of the arts, and curious thinkers seeking profound depth rather than standard corporate polish. The interface must evoke a sense of quiet cosmic awe, scholarly solitude, and architectural exactitude—reminiscent of looking through a deep-space observatory telescope while leafing through a rare illuminated manuscript.

The visual style blends **Deep Cosmic Minimalism** with **Ethereal Glassmorphism**:
- Obsidian and deep void backdrops evoke infinite space and contemplative silence.
- Luminescent edge-lighting, subtle quantum cyan halos, and spiritual amber resonances act as intentional focal points.
- Geometric rigor and razor-sharp typographic hierarchy mirror formal logic and code structures, tempered by delicate literary serifs that honor the poetic traditions of mystic thought.

## Colors

The palette operates under an uncompromising dark mode anchored in deep spatial resonance and intentional luminescence.

- **Primary (`#22D3EE` / Quantum Turquoise):** Embodies the scientific, analytical, and computational layer. Used for primary focus indicators, active quantum states, primary interactive triggers, and data visualizations.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` / Spiritual Amber Resonance):** Reflects the mystical illumination of Sufism, sacred geometry, and warmth in human experience. Reserved for contemplative accents, thematic highlights, audio-musical states, and philosophical quotations.
- **Tertiary (`#8B5CF6` / Starlight Violet):** Bridges the rational and the transcendental. Used sparingly for transitions, ambient background gradients, and deep speculative thought tags.
- **Neutral (`#07090E` / Deep Void Black):** The canvas itself. Provides absolute contrast against which light phenomena take physical weight. Paired with secondary surface tiers: `#0C101A` (Starlight Dark Surface) and `#131B2E` (Starlight Indigo Plate).

Text tiers follow precise optical contrast:
- `text-primary`: `#F8FAFC` (pure starlight crispness)
- `text-secondary`: `#94A3B8` (subdued spectral slate)
- `text-tertiary`: `#475569` (deep field gray)
- `border-subtle`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- `border-luminous`: `rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.35)`

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances academic weight with modern engineering clarity.

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Convey philosophical depth, timeless literature, and meditative presence. Use italics sparingly for poetic verses, Sufic translations, or ontological axioms. Keep tracking slightly compressed on large sizes to preserve editorial elegance.
- **Narrative & Exposition (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers effortless readability for long-form essays and documentation. Its clean geometric curves keep the layout approachable without distracting from the editorial headers.
- **Technical & Metrology (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to formulas, code fragments, date metadata, taxonomy badges, status indicators, and coordinate markers. All labels use uppercase tracking to evoke terminal instrumentation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetrical 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a maximum content container of `1280px` to maintain focused, editorial line lengths.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, `1.5rem` gutters, `3rem` margins. Essays and thesis pieces utilize an offset 7-column reading column flanked by metadata, quantum footnotes, and musical score callouts.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, `1.25rem` gutters, `2rem` margins. Side panels collapse into progressive disclosures or floating anchor drawers.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column grid, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` outer margins. Dynamic vertical spacing compresses gracefully while maintaining ample breathing room between prose sections.

Vertical rhythm relies on generous `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` separators between contrasting disciplines (e.g., transitioning from a software architecture case study into a Sufi meditation on non-duality).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use harsh drop shadows; instead, it relies on **photonic depth, surface absorption, and translucent tiering**:

- **Ground Tier (The Void):** `#07090E` solid background. Absorptive, noise-grained at 2% opacity to eliminate flat screen deadness.
- **Level 1 (Substratum Glass):** `background: rgba(19, 27, 46, 0.45); backdrop-filter: blur(16px); border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06);`. Used for structural panels, code blocks, and card surfaces.
- **Level 2 (Elevated Focal Glass):** `background: rgba(19, 27, 46, 0.7); backdrop-filter: blur(24px); border: 1px solid rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.2); box-shadow: 0 0 25px -5px rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.12);`. Used for active controls, modals, and spotlight essays.
- **Quantum Halo & Aura Effects:** Dynamic radiant highlights. A hover state never simply lifts an element along the Z-axis; it intensifies the perimeter glow with a diffuse `box-shadow: 0 0 30px rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.18)` or gold luminescence `box-shadow: 0 0 30px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (`1`)** roundedness profile to project architectural discipline and scientific apparatus precision:

- Standard cards, interactive inputs, and containers carry a subtle `4px` (`0.25rem`) radius.
- Larger overlays, structural modal windows, and glass viewports use `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- Strict rectangular discipline is maintained for analytical tables, code sandboxes, and audio waveform containers to reflect rigorous calculation.
- Circular or pill geometry is strictly prohibited except for micro-status indicators (e.g., active telemetry pulses or discrete category dots).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary (Quantum Luminary):** Translucent cyan core (`rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.12)`), solid border in `rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.4)`, text in `#22D3EE`. On hover, the background brightens to `rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.2)` with a soft cyan radial aura.
- **Secondary (Contemplative Outline):** Border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text in `#F8FAFC`. On hover, border shifts to `#F59E0B` (Amber Resonance) with text transitioning to spiritual gold.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** `JetBrains Mono` label with a preceding coordinate symbol (`// 01`) and an underline that expands outward from center on hover.

### Chips & Taxonomies
- Minimalist terminal tags formatted in uppercase `label-sm`.
- Styled with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` fill and fine `1px` border matching the thematic domain:
  - *Quantum / Physics:* Bordered in subtle turquoise.
  - *Tasavvuf / Philosophy:* Bordered in subtle amber.
  - *Music / Sound Design:* Bordered in subtle violet.

### Inputs & Terminal Queries
- Surface in `rgba(7, 9, 14, 0.8)`, inset border of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Focus state eliminates standard OS rings, replacing them with a crisp `#22D3EE` border and an internal starlight indigo gradient glow. Placeholder text set in muted slate (`#475569`) with `JetBrains Mono`.

### Cards & Content Viewports
- Layered glass containers with fine `1px` gradient borders (`border-image` fading from faint white to cyan or amber).
- Card titles utilize `Playfair Display`, followed by an ultra-thin divider line `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`, transitioning to technical body text in `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- Custom squared controls (`4px` radius) with `JetBrains Mono` labels.
- Active states glow with a central cyan or amber quantum dot instead of generic checkmarks, echoing particle field visualizations.

### Polymath-Specific Components
- **Aphorism Box:** Centered luxury serif quotation block with an amber left-accent bar that breathes with an ambient CSS pulse animation.
- **Acoustic Waveform Player:** Dark-line visualizer displaying algorithmic synth compositions and modal music scales, integrated with turquoise timeline scrubbers.
- **Interactive Concept Graph Node:** Compact glass pill that exposes systemic relations between metaphysical concepts and computational logic when hovered.
